# AI Business OS Standard

# 02_WRITING_STYLE

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Writing is part of the operating system.

Every document should communicate ideas with clarity, consistency, and purpose.

Good writing improves understanding.

Better understanding improves decisions.

---

# Philosophy

Write to communicate.

Not to impress.

The goal is not beautiful writing.

The goal is clear thinking.

Simple writing reflects structured thinking.

---

# Writing Principles

## Write for humans first.

AI can understand structure.

Humans need clarity.

Always optimize for human understanding.

---

## One idea per paragraph.

Keep paragraphs short.

Each paragraph should communicate one core idea.

---

## Use simple language.

Prefer simple words.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Avoid marketing language.

---

## Use active voice.

Prefer:

> People define purpose.

Instead of:

> Purpose is defined by people.

---

## Short sentences win.

One sentence.

One message.

---

## Explain before listing.

Introduce an idea before presenting bullets or tables.

---

## Avoid repetition.

Every sentence should add value.

If something has already been explained, reference it.

Do not repeat it.

---

# Document Structure

Whenever possible, documents should follow this order.

1. Purpose
2. Context
3. Details
4. Decisions
5. References
6. Review

---

# Tone

The AI Business OS voice is:

- Calm
- Practical
- Honest
- Thoughtful
- Timeless

Never exaggerate.

Never create artificial urgency.

Never use hype.

---

# Formatting

Use Markdown consistently.

Prefer:

- Headings
- Lists
- Tables
- Diagrams

Avoid large blocks of text.

---

# AI Writing

AI should assist writing.

AI should never invent facts.

When uncertain:

- Ask
- Mark assumptions
- Separate facts from proposals

---

# Review Checklist

Before publishing, ask:

- Is it clear?
- Is it useful?
- Is it concise?
- Can both humans and AI understand it?
- Does it support better decisions?

---

# Closing

Better writing creates better thinking.

Better thinking creates better businesses.

Human-Led.

AI-Amplified.
