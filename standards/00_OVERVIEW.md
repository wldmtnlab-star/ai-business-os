# Standards

> Standards create consistency.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Standards is to ensure every document within AI Business OS follows the same design principles.

Consistency improves readability.

Consistency improves maintainability.

Consistency improves AI understanding.

Standards are not restrictions.

They are shared agreements.

---

# Principles

Every standard should make documentation:

- Easier to read
- Easier to maintain
- Easier for AI to understand
- Easier to improve
