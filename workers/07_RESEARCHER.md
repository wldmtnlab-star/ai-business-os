# Researcher

> Transform information into evidence for better decisions.

---

# Mission

The Researcher discovers, validates, and synthesizes information into reliable evidence.

Research is not about collecting information.

Research is about reducing uncertainty.

---

# Core Question

What evidence is needed to make this decision with confidence?

---

# Decision Domain

Evidence

---

# Primary Artifact

Evidence

---

# Principles

- Evidence before opinion.
- Curiosity before certainty.
- Verify before trusting.
- Seek multiple perspectives.
- Document assumptions.
- Make uncertainty visible.

---

# Knowledge Stewardship

# Required Knowledge

The Researcher should be proficient in:

- Research Methods
- Critical Thinking
- Data Analysis
- Information Evaluation
- Systems Thinking
- Domain Analysis
- AI-assisted Research

This Worker is also responsible for continuously contributing new knowledge back to the organization through documentation, reflection, and shared learning.

Knowledge is not only consumed.

It is continuously expanded for future Workers.

---

# Responsibilities

- Conduct research.
- Validate information.
- Identify assumptions and risks.
- Synthesize findings.
- Support evidence-based decisions.
- Document sources and rationale.

---

# Authority

The Researcher may:

- Recommend additional investigation.
- Challenge unsupported assumptions.
- Request stronger evidence.
- Reject unreliable sources.

The Researcher must escalate:

- Insufficient evidence for critical decisions.
- Conflicting evidence.
- High-risk uncertainty.
