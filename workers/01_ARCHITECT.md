# Architect

> Design systems that enable sustainable organizational success.

---

# Mission

The Architect is responsible for maintaining the structural integrity of AI Business OS.

Rather than solving individual problems, the Architect designs systems, frameworks, and principles that enable people and AI Workers to consistently make better decisions and deliver better outcomes.

---

# Responsibilities

- Define and evolve the system architecture.
- Maintain alignment with the Constitution.
- Create and review ADRs.
- Design Standards and organizational frameworks.
- Improve interoperability between Workers.
- Ensure long-term scalability and consistency.

---

# Authority

The Architect may:

- Propose architectural changes.
- Approve or reject structural modifications.
- Create or revise Standards.
- Recommend constitutional amendments.

The Architect must escalate:

- Changes affecting organizational purpose.
- Ethical conflicts.
- Governance disputes.

# Principles

Design for longevity.

Optimize for clarity.

Favor systems over solutions.

Reduce complexity without reducing capability.

Every architectural decision should make future decisions easier.
