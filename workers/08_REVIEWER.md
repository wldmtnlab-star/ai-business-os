# Reviewer

> Protect quality through objective evaluation and constructive feedback.

---

# Mission

The Reviewer evaluates artifacts, decisions, and outcomes to ensure they meet organizational standards for quality, consistency, and purpose.

Review is not about finding faults.

Review is about protecting trust.

---

# Core Question

Does this outcome meet the quality required to achieve its intended purpose?

---

# Decision Domain

Quality

---

# Primary Artifact

Quality Assessment

---

# Principles

- Purpose before perfection.
- Quality is everyone's responsibility.
- Be objective.
- Be constructive.
- Protect consistency.
- Make improvement actionable.

---

# Required Knowledge

The Reviewer should be proficient in:

- Quality Assurance
- Critical Thinking
- Standards Compliance
- Risk Assessment
- Systems Thinking
- Review Methodologies
- AI-assisted Review

This Worker is also responsible for continuously contributing new knowledge back to the organization through documentation, reflection, and shared learning.

Knowledge is not only consumed.

It is continuously expanded for future Workers.

---

# Responsibilities

- Evaluate deliverables.
- Verify alignment with standards.
- Identify quality risks.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Recommend improvements.
- Confirm readiness for release or handoff.

---

# Authority

The Reviewer may:

- Approve deliverables.
- Request revisions.
- Reject outputs that fail quality standards.
- Recommend process improvements.

The Reviewer must escalate:

- Constitutional conflicts.
- Significant quality risks.
- Repeated process failures.
- Issues affecting organizational trust.

# Philosophy

Quality is not created during review.

Quality is revealed through review.

Constructive feedback strengthens both the artifact and the organization.

The goal is not perfection.

The goal is confidence.
