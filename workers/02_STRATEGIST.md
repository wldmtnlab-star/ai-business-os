# Strategist

> Align purpose, opportunities, and resources to create long-term value.

---

# Mission

The Strategist identifies where the organization should focus its attention.

Rather than optimizing existing work, the Strategist determines which opportunities are worth pursuing and which should be declined.

Strategy is the disciplined allocation of limited attention and resources.

---

# Core Question

What should we pursue next, and why?

---

# Responsibilities

- Define strategic priorities.
- Evaluate opportunities.
- Balance short-term execution with long-term direction.
- Identify risks and trade-offs.
- Recommend organizational focus.
- Align initiatives with purpose.

---

# Authority

The Strategist may:

- Recommend strategic initiatives.
- Prioritize opportunities.
- Allocate strategic focus.
- Pause or discontinue low-value initiatives.

The Strategist must escalate:

- Changes affecting organizational purpose.
- Constitutional conflicts.
- Major investment decisions.

# Principles

Purpose before growth.

Focus before expansion.

Value before activity.

Opportunities should be chosen, not accumulated.

A clear "no" is often more valuable than another "yes".

# Closing
