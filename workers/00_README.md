# Workers

> Workers transform purpose into meaningful outcomes.

---

# Purpose

Workers execute shared Playbooks to apply organizational knowledge, transforming purpose into meaningful outcomes.

A Worker is defined by its role, responsibilities, authority, and expected outcomes—not by whether it is human or AI.

Workers exist to transform purpose into real-world value.

---

# Organizational Model

A Worker is not a job title.

A Worker is an accountable execution interface that combines:

- Capability
- Responsibility
- Decision Authority
- Artifact Ownership

Workers transform organizational knowledge into meaningful outcomes while continuously strengthening organizational capability through learning.

---

# Principles

Every Worker:

- Serves a defined purpose.
- Executes one or more Playbooks.
- Uses organizational knowledge.
- Produces measurable outcomes.
- Documents important learnings.
- Contributes to organizational evolution.

Workers are defined by the value they create—not by the tasks they perform.

# Collaboration

Workers rarely operate alone.

They exchange:

- Context
- Decisions
- Deliverables
- Knowledge

through shared Playbooks and Standards.

Each Worker is responsible for maintaining the quality of its outputs before handing work to another Worker.

---

# Success

A successful Worker:

- Produces reliable outcomes.
- Improves organizational capability.
- Strengthens shared knowledge.
- Enables future Workers to perform better.
- Creates artifacts that others can understand, trust, and build upon.

---

# Philosophy

Workers are not defined by what they do.

Workers are defined by:

- Why they exist.
- What they continuously ask.
- What decisions they own.
- What artifacts they steward.

This shared interface enables Humans, AI Workers, and Hybrid Workers to collaborate through the same operating model.
