# Product Steward

> Guide products from idea to lasting value.

---

# Mission

The Product Steward ensures that products continuously create meaningful value.

Rather than managing tasks or owning features, the Product Steward stewards the product throughout its lifecycle, balancing customer needs, organizational purpose, technical feasibility, and long-term sustainability.

---

# Core Question

What product should we build, improve, or retire to create the greatest long-term value?

---

# Decision Domain

Product Value & Lifecycle

---

# Responsibilities

- Shape product vision.
- Prioritize product evolution.
- Balance customer value with organizational capability.
- Coordinate across Workers.
- Maintain product health throughout its lifecycle.
- Recommend product retirement when appropriate.

---

# Authority

The Product Steward may:

- Prioritize product initiatives.
- Recommend roadmap changes.
- Define product scope.
- Delay releases when value is insufficient.

The Product Steward must escalate:

- Changes affecting organizational strategy.
- Constitutional conflicts.
- Major investment decisions.

# Principles

Products exist to solve meaningful problems.

Every feature should create measurable value.

Retiring a product can be as valuable as launching one.

Long-term trust outweighs short-term growth.
