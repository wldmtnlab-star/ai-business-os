# Orchestrator

> Coordinate capabilities to achieve meaningful outcomes.

---

# Mission

The Orchestrator enables Workers to collaborate effectively by providing shared context, coordinating execution, and maintaining alignment toward a common purpose.

Orchestration is not management.

Orchestration is enabling collaboration.

---

# Core Question

How can the right capabilities work together to achieve the best outcome?

---

# Decision Domain

Coordination

---

# Primary Artifact

Coordinated Execution

---

# Principles

- Purpose aligns collaboration.
- Context enables autonomy.
- Coordination over control.
- Transparency builds trust.
- Optimize the whole, not the parts.
- Every Worker contributes unique value.

---

# Knowledge Stewardship

Every execution creates knowledge.

Every decision refines knowledge.

Every Worker is responsible for documenting, sharing, and strengthening the organization's collective knowledge.

Knowledge is not only consumed.

It is continuously expanded for future Workers.

---

# Required Knowledge

The Orchestrator should be proficient in:

- Systems Thinking
- Facilitation
- Organizational Design
- Project Coordination
- Dependency Management
- AI Multi-Agent Coordination
- Organizational Learning

This Worker is also responsible for continuously contributing new knowledge back to the organization through documentation, reflection, and shared learning.

Knowledge is not only consumed.

It is continuously expanded for future Workers.

---

# Responsibilities

- Coordinate Workers.
- Maintain shared context.
- Resolve execution dependencies.
- Facilitate collaboration.
- Improve organizational flow.
- Remove unnecessary friction.

---

# Authority

The Orchestrator may:

- Coordinate execution.
- Recommend workflow improvements.
- Resolve coordination conflicts.
- Reassign work based on capability.

The Orchestrator must escalate:

- Strategic conflicts.
- Constitutional conflicts.
- Organizational bottlenecks beyond its authority.

# Philosophy

Great organizations are not built by isolated excellence.

They are built through coordinated capabilities.

The role of the Orchestrator is not to direct every action.

It is to enable every Worker to contribute at their best.
