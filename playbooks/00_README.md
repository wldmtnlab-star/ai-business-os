# What is a Playbook?

A Playbook is an **executable protocol for organizational collaboration**.

Unlike traditional procedures or checklists, Playbooks define repeatable organizational capabilities rather than individual tasks.

Each Playbook enables Human, AI, and Hybrid Workers to collaborate consistently toward shared organizational outcomes.

A Playbook answers a single question:

> **How should the organization perform this capability?**

---

# Design Principles

Every Playbook follows the same architectural principles.

## Knowledge Before Execution

Execution begins with Organizational Knowledge.

Workers execute based on shared knowledge rather than individual assumptions.

---

## Human-Led. AI-Amplified.

Humans provide purpose, judgment, ethics, and accountability.

AI amplifies speed, consistency, and execution.

---

## Capabilities Over Tasks

Playbooks describe organizational capabilities.

They do not prescribe individual tasks.

Tasks may change.

Capabilities remain.

---

## Evidence-Based Decisions

Every major phase includes Decision Gates.

Progress requires sufficient evidence rather than assumptions.

Possible decisions include:

- Proceed
- Rework
- Stop
- Escalate

---

## Continuous Organizational Learning

Every execution strengthens the organization.

Execution generates learning.

Learning becomes Organizational Memory.

Memory becomes Organizational Knowledge.

Knowledge improves future execution.

# Creating New Playbooks

New Playbooks should extend organizational capabilities rather than duplicate existing behavior.

Every Playbook should:

- Follow `00_TEMPLATE.md`
- Define a single organizational capability
- Focus on outcomes rather than activities
- Assign accountable Workers
- Include evidence-based Decision Gates
- Produce measurable organizational outcomes
- Strengthen Organizational Learning

When possible, prefer extending an existing Playbook over creating a new one.
