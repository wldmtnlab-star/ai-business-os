# AIBOS Charter

This Charter defines the guiding principles of the AIBOS project.

---

# Mission

To establish an open specification for designing, sharing, and executing AI-native organizations.

---

# Vision

Organizations should be:

- Human-Led
- AI-Amplified
- Open
- Reusable
- Versioned

---

# Our Values

## Specification First

Specifications come before implementations.

A specification enables multiple runtimes,
multiple programming languages,
and long-term interoperability.

---

## Human First

Humans remain responsible for decisions.

AI assists.

Humans lead.

---

## Open by Default

Specifications should be public whenever possible.

Knowledge grows through collaboration.

---

## Runtime Agnostic

AIBOS does not belong to a single AI provider.

Implementations may use:

- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google
- Local Models
- Future Models

---

## Components over Monoliths

Small reusable components are preferred over large monolithic systems.

Knowledge.

Workers.

Playbooks.

Organizations.

---

## Simplicity

AIBOS should remain understandable.

Simple systems survive.

Complexity should emerge from composition,
not from the core specification.

---

## Backward Compatibility

Breaking changes should be avoided.

When unavoidable,
they must introduce a new apiVersion.

---

## Community

AIBOS belongs to everyone.

No implementation owns the specification.

---

# Governance

Major specification changes should be discussed publicly.

Consensus is preferred.

Compatibility is valued.

---

# Non Goals

AIBOS is not:

- an AI model
- a chatbot
- a workflow SaaS
- an automation platform
- a replacement for humans

AIBOS is a specification.

---

# Motto

Human-Led.

AI-Amplified.

Organizations as Code.
