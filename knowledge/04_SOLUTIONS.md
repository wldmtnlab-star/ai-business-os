# Purpose

This document defines the official solution portfolio of the organization.

A solution combines products, knowledge, processes, and human expertise to solve a meaningful business problem.

Solutions answer **"Why do customers choose us?"**

# Solution Design Principles

Every solution should:

- Solve a real business problem.
- Be repeatable.
- Combine products and expertise.
- Produce measurable outcomes.
- Improve through experience.

---

# Relationship Model

```text

↓

Solutions

↓

Services
```

Products provide capabilities.
