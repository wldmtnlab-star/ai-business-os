# Services

> The services offered by the organization to deliver customer value.

# Purpose

This document defines the official service portfolio of the organization.

A service is the structured delivery of expertise that creates measurable customer outcomes.

Services transform solutions into real-world results through consulting, implementation, design, development, education, and ongoing support.

# Service Delivery Principles

Every service should:

- Solve a meaningful customer problem.
- Follow repeatable processes.
- Produce measurable outcomes.
- Generate reusable knowledge.
- Improve continuously.
