# Brands

> The portfolio of brands operated by the organization.

---

# Purpose

This document defines every official brand managed within this AI Business OS implementation.

A brand is more than a logo.

A brand represents a promise, an identity, and a relationship with its audience.

AI Workers should understand the purpose of each brand before making decisions related to communication, design, or strategy.

# Brand Principles

Every brand should:

- Have a clear purpose.
- Serve a distinct audience.
- Maintain a consistent identity.
- Contribute knowledge back to the operating system.

---

# Future Brands

New brands should only be created when they have:

- A unique purpose.
- A clearly defined audience.
- A sustainable operating model.
- A meaningful relationship with AI Business OS.
