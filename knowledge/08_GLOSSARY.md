# Glossary

> Shared language for humans and AI.

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout AI Business OS.

Every important concept should have exactly one canonical definition.

When humans and AI use the same language, they make better decisions.

---

# Organization

## Company

The organization operating AI Business OS.

---

## Brand

The identity and promise presented to the outside world.

Brands build trust.

---

## Product

A repeatable value delivery system.

Products provide capabilities.

---

## Solution

A structured approach to solving a business problem.

Solutions combine products, knowledge, and expertise.

---

## Service

The structured delivery of expertise that creates measurable customer outcomes.

Services deliver solutions.

---

## Project

A temporary initiative with a defined objective.

Projects generate new knowledge.

---

## Knowledge

Structured organizational understanding that improves decision-making.

Knowledge survives beyond individuals.

---

## AI Worker

An AI entity with a clearly defined role, responsibilities, authority, and operating context.

AI Workers collaborate with humans rather than replace them.

---

## Human

The final decision-maker.

Humans provide judgment, ethics, creativity, and accountability.

---

## Constitution

The highest operational authority of AI Business OS.

Every AI Worker and human contributor should operate within its principles.

---

## Playbook

A repeatable workflow for performing a specific task.

Playbooks improve consistency and quality.

---

## Standard

A rule that defines how work should be performed.

Standards improve interoperability and maintainability.

---

## Knowledge Layer

The structured memory of the organization.

It enables humans and AI to share understanding.

---

## Reference Implementation

A practical example demonstrating how the framework should be applied.

mtn-ai-os is the official reference implementation of AI Business OS.

---

# Principles

Every important concept should have:

- One definition.
- One meaning.
- One source of truth.

Avoid synonyms when precision matters.

---

# Terminology Rules

When introducing a new concept:

1. Define it.
2. Add it to this glossary.
3. Use it consistently.

If a definition changes, update this document first.
