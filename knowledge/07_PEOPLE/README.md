# People

> The people who contribute to the organization and the roles they perform.

---

# Purpose

People are not documented as personal profiles.

They are documented through the capabilities, responsibilities, and knowledge they contribute to the organization.

The goal is to make organizational knowledge transferable rather than dependent on individuals.

---

# Principles

Every person should have:

- A clearly defined role.
- Defined responsibilities.
- Areas of expertise.
- Decision authority.
- Current projects.

---

# Organization Philosophy

People change.

Knowledge remains.

Roles evolve.

The operating system should continue to function regardless of personnel changes.
