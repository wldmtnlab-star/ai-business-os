# Projects

> Active and historical initiatives executed by the organization.

---

# Purpose

Projects represent the execution layer of the organization.

They apply services, solutions, products, and organizational knowledge to achieve specific goals.

Unlike Products or Services, projects are temporary.

Every project should contribute new knowledge back to the organization.

---

# Lifecycle

```text
Idea

↓

Planning

↓

Active

↓

Review

↓

Completed

↓

Knowledge Archived
```

Every completed project strengthens the Knowledge Layer.

---

# Principles

Every project should:

- Have a clear objective.
- Define measurable outcomes.
- Produce reusable knowledge.
- Document important decisions.
- Record lessons learned.
