# ADR-0004

# Shared Playbooks

## Context

Organizational knowledge alone is insufficient to produce consistent execution.

Knowledge explains *what* and *why*, but does not consistently describe *how* work should be performed.

A shared execution model is required so that Humans, AI Workers, and Hybrid Workers can perform work consistently.

---

## Decision

Playbooks are adopted as the shared execution layer of AI Business OS.

Playbooks transform organizational knowledge into repeatable organizational capabilities.

Workers execute Playbooks to produce meaningful outcomes.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Consistent execution across Workers.
- Shared organizational practices.
- Reusable execution patterns.
- Improved collaboration between Humans and AI.
- Continuous organizational learning.

### Trade-offs

- Playbooks require continuous maintenance.
- Organizational practices must evolve over time.

---

## Principle

Knowledge explains.

Playbooks operationalize.

Workers execute.
