# ADR-0012

# Evidence Precedes Confidence

## Context

Research exists to reduce uncertainty, not to confirm assumptions.

As AI systems become increasingly capable of generating persuasive explanations and recommendations, there is a growing risk of assigning confidence without sufficient supporting evidence.

AI Business OS requires a research model that distinguishes evidence from opinion, intuition, and assertion.

Reliable execution depends on the quality of evidence rather than the certainty of conclusions.

---

## Decision

Evidence shall be collected, evaluated, and documented before confidence is assigned.

The Researcher Worker is responsible for assessing the strength, relevance, and reliability of available evidence.

Confidence must always reflect the quality of evidence rather than the confidence of the researcher, stakeholder, or AI system.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Research becomes transparent and reproducible.
- Decisions are supported by traceable evidence.
- AI-generated recommendations become more trustworthy.
- Organizational bias is reduced.
- Uncertainty is communicated honestly.

### Trade-offs

- Strong evidence may require additional time and effort.
- Some decisions must proceed despite incomplete evidence.
- Research standards require continuous improvement.

---

## Principle

Evidence informs.

Confidence reflects evidence.

Uncertainty deserves transparency.

---

Evidence should guide confidence before confidence guides action.
