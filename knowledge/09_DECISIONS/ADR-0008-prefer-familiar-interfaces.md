# ADR-0008

# Prefer Familiar Interfaces

## Context

The adoption of new technology is often limited by the need for users to learn entirely new interfaces and workflows.

Organizations already operate through familiar tools such as chat platforms, documentation systems, issue trackers, project management software, and development environments.

AI Business OS is designed to augment existing ways of working rather than replace them.

To maximize adoption and minimize friction, AI capabilities should integrate naturally into established workflows.

---

## Decision

AI Business OS shall prioritize familiar interfaces over introducing proprietary user experiences.

Workers should be accessible through the tools people already use whenever practical.

The operating model is defined by shared capabilities and Playbooks, not by a specific application or interface.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Faster organizational adoption.
- Reduced learning curve.
- Lower resistance to AI integration.
- Easier integration with existing toolchains.
- Greater flexibility across organizations.

### Trade-offs

- User experience may be constrained by existing platforms.
- Some advanced capabilities may require platform-specific implementations.
- Consistency across multiple interfaces requires additional design effort.

---

## Principle

Capabilities transcend interfaces.

Interfaces enable adoption.

Familiarity accelerates transformation.

---

Innovation succeeds when it feels familiar.
