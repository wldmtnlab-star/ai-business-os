# ADR-0015

# Reference Organizations Demonstrate the Framework

## Context

AI Business OS is an organizational framework rather than a prescriptive methodology.

Organizations should adapt the framework to their own context instead of copying predefined implementations.

Examples are therefore needed to demonstrate how the framework can be applied without becoming the framework itself.

---

## Decision

Reference Organizations are adopted as practical implementations of AI Business OS.

Each Reference Organization demonstrates how the framework can be adapted to a specific organization, industry, or operating model.

Reference Organizations are examples.

They are not templates.

Organizations are encouraged to study, adapt, and extend them according to their own purpose and context.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Encourages adaptation instead of imitation.
- Demonstrates real-world implementation.
- Supports multiple industries and organization types.
- Enables community contributions without changing the core framework.

### Trade-offs

- Reference Organizations require ongoing maintenance.
- Example implementations may evolve at different speeds.

---

## Principle

The framework defines principles.

Reference Organizations demonstrate implementation.

Every organization builds its own operating system.
