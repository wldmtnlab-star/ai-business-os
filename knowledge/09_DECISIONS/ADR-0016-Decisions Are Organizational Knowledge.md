# ADR-0016

# Decisions Are Organizational Knowledge

## Context

Organizations make important decisions every day.

When those decisions remain undocumented, the reasoning behind them is eventually forgotten.

Future contributors repeat discussions, revisit resolved questions, and lose valuable organizational context.

AI Business OS treats knowledge as a strategic organizational asset.

Important decisions should therefore become part of the Knowledge Layer.

---

## Decision

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) are adopted as reusable organizational knowledge.

An ADR records not only what was decided, but why the decision was made, which alternatives were considered, and what consequences are expected.

ADRs preserve organizational reasoning so future contributors can understand and extend previous work without rediscovering it.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Organizational reasoning is preserved.
- Architectural consistency improves.
- Contributors understand historical context.
- Repeated discussions are reduced.
- Knowledge compounds over time.

### Trade-offs

- Significant decisions require additional documentation.
- ADRs must be reviewed when architectural assumptions change.

---

## Principle

Decisions preserve reasoning.

Reasoning creates knowledge.

Knowledge improves future decisions.
