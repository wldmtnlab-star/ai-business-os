# ADR-0007

# Playbooks Define Organizational Capabilities

## Context

Organizations often document procedures, workflows, and best practices.

However, static procedures alone do not create organizational capability.

A capability emerges when knowledge is transformed into a repeatable approach that can be executed consistently by Humans, AI Workers, and Hybrid Workers.

AI Business OS requires a shared execution model that captures not only *how* work is performed, but also *why* it exists and *how* it continuously evolves.

---

## Decision

Playbooks are adopted as the primary representation of organizational capabilities.

A Playbook defines the shared practices, principles, and decision models that enable consistent execution across Workers.

Workers execute Playbooks to transform organizational knowledge into meaningful outcomes.

Playbooks are living organizational assets that continuously evolve through execution and learning.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Organizational capabilities become explicit and reusable.
- Execution becomes consistent across Workers.
- Collaboration improves through shared practices.
- Continuous improvement becomes part of daily execution.
- AI Workers and Human Workers share the same operational model.

### Trade-offs

- Playbooks require continuous refinement.
- Organizational capabilities must be reviewed as the organization evolves.
- Maintaining Playbooks requires discipline and governance.

---

## Principle

Knowledge explains.

Playbooks operationalize.

Workers execute.

Capabilities evolve.
