# ADR-0013

# Organizational Learning Loop

## Context

Organizations improve not through isolated successes, but through their ability to continuously learn from execution.

Execution produces artifacts, artifacts become organizational memory, and organizational memory informs future knowledge, playbooks, and execution.

Without an intentional learning loop, organizations repeatedly solve the same problems, lose valuable experience, and fail to accumulate capability over time.

AI Business OS requires a continuous feedback mechanism that transforms execution into organizational evolution.

---

## Decision

AI Business OS adopts the Organizational Learning Loop as its continuous improvement model.

The Organizational Learning Loop connects purpose, knowledge, execution, and learning into a self-reinforcing cycle.

Purpose guides execution.

Execution produces artifacts.

Artifacts become organizational memory.

Organizational memory strengthens future knowledge and playbooks.

Improved knowledge enables better execution, completing the learning loop.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Organizational capability continuously improves.
- Knowledge compounds through repeated execution.
- Organizational memory becomes a strategic asset.
- Workers continuously benefit from previous learning.
- AI and Humans improve together through shared organizational learning.

### Trade-offs

- Organizational learning requires disciplined documentation.
- Learning loops require active maintenance and governance.
- Outdated knowledge must be continuously reviewed and refined.

---

## Principle

Purpose inspires execution.

Execution creates artifacts.

Artifacts preserve learning.

Learning strengthens purpose.

---

Organizations evolve by continuously learning from execution.
