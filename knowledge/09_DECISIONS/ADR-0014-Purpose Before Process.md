# ADR-0014

# Purpose Before Process

## Context

Organizations often begin by designing processes, workflows, and tools.

While these elements improve execution, they cannot provide direction on their own.

Without a clearly defined purpose, processes become isolated optimizations that may improve efficiency without improving organizational outcomes.

AI Business OS requires a stable foundation that guides every subsequent architectural decision.

---

## Decision

Purpose is established as the highest architectural layer of AI Business OS.

Every organizational component—including the Constitution, Operating Model, Knowledge, Playbooks, Workers, and Execution—must ultimately support the organization's purpose.

Processes exist to serve purpose.

Purpose never exists to serve process.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Organizational decisions remain aligned.
- Execution reflects long-term intent rather than short-term optimization.
- AI recommendations remain grounded in organizational purpose.
- Future architectural changes can be evaluated consistently.

### Trade-offs

- Purpose requires deliberate definition and periodic reflection.
- Organizations may need to redesign existing processes that no longer support their purpose.

---

## Principle

Purpose defines direction.

Processes enable execution.

Optimization without purpose creates complexity.
