# Products

> The products developed, maintained, or operated by the organization.

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official product portfolio of the organization.

A product is a repeatable value delivery system.

Products may be open source, commercial, digital, physical, or AI-powered.

Every product should have a clearly defined purpose, audience, lifecycle, and relationship to the organization's brands.

# Product Lifecycle

Every product progresses through the following lifecycle.

```text
Idea

↓

Planning

↓

Development

↓

Launch

↓

Growth

↓

Maintenance

↓

Retired
```

Products evolve continuously.

Retirement is part of a healthy portfolio.

---

# Product Principles

Every product should:

- Solve a real problem.
- Have a clearly defined audience.
- Deliver measurable value.
- Generate knowledge.
- Improve continuously.
- Support the long-term vision.

---

# Relationship to Brands

Brands communicate identity.

Products deliver value.

One brand may contain multiple products.

One product belongs to one primary brand.

---

# Relationship to Solutions

Products enable solutions.

Solutions combine one or more products with expertise to solve customer problems.
