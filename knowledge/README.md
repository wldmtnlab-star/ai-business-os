# Knowledge Layer

> **The organizational memory of AI Business OS.**

---

# Purpose

Knowledge is the most valuable asset of a modern business.

Products change.

Technology evolves.

People come and go.

Knowledge should remain.

The Knowledge Layer exists to preserve, organize, and continuously improve what an organization knows.

It is the collective memory of the business.

---

# Philosophy

Knowledge is not documentation.

Knowledge is decision-making.

Every decision should become knowledge.

Every project should create knowledge.

Every lesson should strengthen the organization.

Knowledge compounds over time.

---

# Principles

## Knowledge belongs to the organization.

No critical knowledge should exist only in one person's mind.

---

## Write for humans first.

AI can read structured information.

Humans need clarity.

Every document should be understandable by both.

---

## Capture decisions, not just information.

Facts explain the past.

Decisions improve the future.

Always record why a decision was made.

---

## Keep knowledge alive.

Knowledge is never finished.

Review it.

Improve it.

Archive what is no longer useful.

---

## One document, one responsibility.

Each document answers one primary question.

Avoid duplication.

Reference instead of copying.

---

# Structure

The Knowledge Layer is organized as follows.

```text
knowledge/

00_README.md

01_COMPANY.md

02_BRANDS.md

03_PRODUCTS.md

04_SERVICES.md

05_PROJECTS.md

06_PEOPLE.md

07_GLOSSARY.md

08_DECISIONS.md

09_HISTORY.md
```

Each document has a clearly defined purpose.

Together they describe the organization.

---

# Relationship to AI Workers

Every AI Worker reads the Knowledge Layer before making decisions.

The decision flow is always:

```text
Constitution

↓

Knowledge Layer

↓

Playbooks

↓

Task
```

This ensures decisions remain consistent with the organization's purpose and principles.

---

# Relationship to People

The Knowledge Layer is not owned by AI.

It belongs to everyone in the organization.

People create knowledge.

AI organizes, expands, and retrieves it.

Humans remain responsible for its accuracy.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Knowledge should become more valuable every week.

Every project should improve it.

Every review should refine it.

Every lesson should be captured.

The organization grows when its knowledge grows.

---

# Closing

Knowledge is not stored for the past.

It is created for the future.

Human-Led.

AI-Amplified.
