# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone, regardless of background, experience, identity, or skill level.

AI Business OS exists to help organizations learn, collaborate, and continuously improve.

Our community should reflect those same values.

---

## Our Values

We encourage:

- Respectful communication
- Constructive feedback
- Curiosity and continuous learning
- Open collaboration
- Evidence-based discussion
- Kindness and professionalism

We believe diverse perspectives make better organizations.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful.
- Be constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Welcome different viewpoints.
- Help others learn.
- Focus on improving ideas rather than criticizing people.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Hate speech
- Trolling
- Deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information without permission

These behaviors are not acceptable in any project space.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub Discussions
- Issues
- Pull Requests
- Community conversations
- Project-related communication

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please open a private discussion with the project maintainers or contact them through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate discretion.

---

## Questions

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers through GitHub.

---

## A Shared Commitment

AI Business OS is more than a collection of documents.

It is a community built around learning, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Every interaction is an opportunity to strengthen that community.

Thank you for helping make AI Business OS welcoming, respectful, and valuable for everyone.
