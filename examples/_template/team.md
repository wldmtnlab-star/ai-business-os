# Team

> Define the people, roles, and responsibilities that bring your organization to life.

A team is more than a collection of job titles.

It is a network of people working together toward a shared purpose.

This document defines the roles, responsibilities, collaboration patterns, and decision-making relationships within your organization.

# Tips

- Design responsibilities before job titles.
- Keep responsibilities clear.
- Encourage collaboration.
- Document knowledge continuously.
- Adapt the team as the organization evolves.

A great team is defined by how well it learns and collaborates—not by the number of people it employs.
