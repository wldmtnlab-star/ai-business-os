# Operating Model

> Define how your organization operates every day.

An Operating Model describes how your organization transforms Purpose into consistent execution.

It establishes the routines, collaboration patterns, and decision-making processes that enable people and AI to work together effectively.

Unlike the Constitution, which defines enduring principles, the Operating Model describes how those principles are put into practice.

---

# Purpose Alignment

Your Operating Model should directly support your organization's Purpose.

Every process, meeting, workflow, and decision-making practice should contribute to achieving that Purpose.

When your organization changes, update the Operating Model—not your Purpose.

---

# Operating Principles

Describe the principles that guide day-to-day operations.

Examples:

- Purpose before process.
- Document knowledge as you work.
- Prefer asynchronous collaboration when possible.
- Keep meetings focused and outcome-oriented.
- Improve systems continuously.

Choose principles that reflect how your organization actually operates.

---

# Daily Rhythm

Describe the daily operating rhythm.

Questions to consider:

- How does work begin each day?
- How are priorities communicated?
- How are blockers identified?
- How is progress shared?

Example:

- Daily planning
- Focused execution
- Knowledge updates
- End-of-day reflection

---

# Weekly Rhythm

Describe recurring weekly activities.

Examples:

- Planning
- Team synchronization
- Reviews
- Retrospectives
- Learning sessions

Focus on activities that improve execution and collaboration.

---

# Project Lifecycle

Describe how projects move from idea to completion.

Example:

```text
Discovery
      │
      ▼
Planning
      │
      ▼
Execution
      │
      ▼
Review
      │
      ▼
Delivery
      │
      ▼
Organizational Learning
```

Projects should always conclude with captured learning.

---

# Knowledge Flow

Describe how knowledge moves through your organization.

Questions to consider:

- Where is knowledge documented?
- How is knowledge reviewed?
- How does learning become reusable?

Knowledge should become an organizational asset—not remain with individuals.

---

# Human + AI Collaboration

Describe how Humans and AI work together.

Questions to consider:

- Which tasks benefit from AI?
- Which decisions remain human-led?
- How is AI output reviewed?
- How is accountability maintained?

AI should amplify capability while humans retain responsibility.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Describe how your organization improves over time.

Examples:

- Retrospectives
- Workflow improvements
- Playbook updates
- Knowledge refinement
- New Case Studies
- ADRs for major decisions

Improvement should be intentional and continuous.

---

# Success Indicators

How will you know your Operating Model is effective?

Examples:

- Faster delivery
- Better collaboration
- Higher quality
- Increased knowledge reuse
- Reduced rework
- Greater customer satisfaction

Measure outcomes rather than activity.

# Tips

- Keep the model simple.
- Focus on how work is actually performed.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Design for adaptability.
- Review and improve regularly.

An Operating Model should help people work better—not create more process.
