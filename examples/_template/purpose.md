# Purpose

> Define why your organization exists.

Purpose is the foundation of AI Business OS.

Everything else—including your Constitution, Operating Model, Services, Workflows, and Organizational Memory—should align with your Purpose.

Purpose should remain stable even as your organization evolves.

---

# Why Your Organization Exists

Describe the reason your organization exists.

Questions to consider:

- Why was your organization created?
- What problem does it solve?
- Who benefits from your work?
- What long-term impact do you hope to create?

Write a concise statement that expresses your organization's reason for existing.

---

# Vision

Describe the future your organization wants to help create.

Questions to consider:

- What does success look like?
- What change do you want to make?
- What future are you working toward?

---

# Mission

Describe how your organization works toward its vision.

Questions to consider:

- What do you do?
- Who do you serve?
- How do you create value?

---

# Core Values

List the principles that guide your organization's behavior.

Examples:

- Transparency
- Collaboration
- Continuous Learning
- Customer Focus
- Craftsmanship
- Simplicity

Choose values that genuinely reflect how your organization operates.

---

# Success Indicators

How will you know your organization is fulfilling its Purpose?

Examples:

- Customer outcomes
- Community impact
- Product quality
- Team growth
- Financial sustainability

Focus on meaningful indicators rather than vanity metrics.

---

# Relationship to AI Business OS

Purpose is the highest level of organizational design.

Everything else should support it.

```text
Purpose
      │
      ▼
Constitution
      │
      ▼
Operating Model
      │
      ▼
Execution
      │
      ▼
Learning
      └────────────────────► Purpose
```

Purpose provides direction.

Learning strengthens Purpose over time.

---

# Tips

- Keep it simple.
- Focus on why, not how.
- Avoid marketing language.
- Make it meaningful.
- Write something your team can remember.

Purpose should inspire decisions—not decorate a website.
