# Services

> Define the repeatable capabilities your organization provides.

Services represent the value your organization consistently delivers.

Rather than listing one-off projects or custom work, describe the repeatable capabilities that fulfill your organization's Purpose.

Each Service should have a clear outcome, a defined scope, and one or more supporting Workflows.

---

# Purpose Alignment

Every Service should directly support your organization's Purpose.

If a Service does not contribute to your Purpose, consider whether it belongs in your organization.

Purpose should drive Services—not the other way around.

---

# Service Portfolio

Describe how your Services relate to one another.

Questions to consider:

- Which Services are core offerings?
- Which Services support others?
- Which Services are experimental?
- Which Services generate long-term value?

Understanding your portfolio helps prioritize organizational effort.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Services should evolve through execution.

Use:

- Case Studies
- Customer feedback
- Retrospectives
- Organizational Memory
- ADRs

to continuously improve how each Service is delivered.

# Tips

- Describe outcomes, not tasks.
- Keep Services repeatable.
- Link every Service to one or more Workflows.
- Improve Services through real-world experience.
- Remove Services that no longer support your Purpose.

Services define what your organization consistently delivers—not everything it is capable of doing.
