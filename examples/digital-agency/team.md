# Team

> Great organizations are built by teams—not individuals.

MTN Digital is organized around cross-functional teams that combine human expertise with AI-assisted execution.

Each team member has clearly defined responsibilities, while AI Workers provide specialized support to improve quality, speed, and organizational learning.

Roles define responsibilities.

Teams create outcomes.

---

```text
                   Client

                      │
      ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
      ▼              ▼              ▼
 Architect       Designer      Writer
      │              │
      └──────────────┼──────────────┘
                     ▼

                     ▼

```

The structure represents collaboration rather than hierarchy.

Depending on the project, one person may fulfill multiple roles, and AI Workers may support multiple team members simultaneously.

# Team Collaboration

Every project is delivered through collaboration rather than isolated expertise.

Each role contributes unique knowledge while sharing responsibility for the final outcome.

Knowledge flows continuously across the team.

Lessons learned become organizational assets.

AI Workers accelerate execution, but accountability always remains with the human team.

---

# Human + AI Collaboration

Human professionals are responsible for:

- Purpose
- Strategy
- Creativity
- Ethics
- Relationships
- Judgment
- Accountability

AI Workers amplify the team's capabilities through:

- Research
- Analysis
- Brainstorming
- Drafting
- Automation
- Knowledge Retrieval
- Documentation

The objective is not to replace people.

The objective is to enable teams to achieve greater impact through collaboration with AI.
