# Constitution

> The principles that govern how MTN Digital thinks, decides, and operates.

The Constitution translates our Purpose into everyday organizational behavior.

It provides a shared foundation for decision-making, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Every playbook, worker, and project aligns with these principles.

---

# Principle 1 — Purpose Before Tasks

We prioritize meaningful outcomes over busy work.

Every initiative should contribute to our organizational purpose.

Before starting any work, we ask:

> Why does this matter?

---

# Principle 2 — Human-Led. AI-Amplified.

AI is a collaborator—not a decision maker.

Humans remain responsible for:

- Purpose
- Ethics
- Judgment
- Accountability

AI enhances execution, analysis, and creativity while humans provide direction.

---

# Principle 3 — Knowledge Before Execution

Work begins with shared understanding.

Before executing, we seek existing knowledge, document new insights, and make information accessible for future reuse.

Knowledge compounds over time.

---

# Principle 4 — Systems Over Heroics

Sustainable organizations rely on repeatable systems rather than individual effort.

We design processes that enable consistent, high-quality outcomes regardless of who performs the work.

---

# Principle 5 — Evidence-Based Decisions

Important decisions should be supported by evidence whenever possible.

Evidence may include:

- Customer feedback
- Data and metrics
- Research
- Previous experience
- Team expertise

Opinions are valuable, but evidence improves decisions.

---

# Principle 6 — Transparency by Default

Knowledge, decisions, and progress should be visible to the organization unless there is a legitimate reason for confidentiality.

Transparency improves trust and organizational learning.

---

# Principle 7 — Continuous Evolution

Every project is an opportunity to improve the organization.

We regularly review:

- What worked
- What didn't
- What we learned
- What should change

Learning is treated as an organizational asset.

---

# Decision Filter

Before making significant decisions, we ask:

- Does this support our purpose?
- Does it create long-term value?
- Can others understand and repeat this approach?
- Does it improve organizational knowledge?
- Will it strengthen future execution?

If the answer is consistently "no," the decision should be reconsidered.

---

# Organizational Commitment

These principles are not aspirational statements.

They define how MTN Digital operates.

Every worker, playbook, project, and decision is expected to align with this Constitution.
