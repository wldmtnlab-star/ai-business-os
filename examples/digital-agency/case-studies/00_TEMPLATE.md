# Case Study Template

> A reusable template for documenting organizational learning through real-world execution.

This template documents how an organization applied AI Business OS to a real-world initiative.

A case study is not simply a project report.

It captures organizational learning by connecting Purpose, Knowledge, Playbooks, Workers, Execution, and Continuous Evolution.
