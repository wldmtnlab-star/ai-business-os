> A complete digital transformation project demonstrating AI Business OS in action.

# Overview

## Project Name

Corporate Website Redesign

## Organization

MTN Digital

## Industry

Digital Agency

## Duration

8 Weeks

## Summary

A client requested a complete redesign of their corporate website to improve usability, strengthen their brand, and increase online inquiries.

The project involved research, UX/UI design, frontend development, content creation, and project delivery.

---

# Challenge

The existing website suffered from several issues:

- Outdated visual design
- Poor mobile experience
- Low conversion rate
- Difficult content management
- Inconsistent branding

The organization needed a modern website that better reflected its business and supported future growth.

- Shared design standards improved consistency.
- AI accelerated content drafting while requiring human review.
- Documentation simplified project handover.
