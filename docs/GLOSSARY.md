# Glossary

> Shared language creates shared understanding.

AI Business OS defines a common language for designing organizations in the AI era.

These definitions ensure that Humans, AI, and Hybrid Workers interpret organizational concepts consistently.

---

# A

## Artifact

A reusable output produced through organizational execution.

Artifacts include documents, decisions, software, designs, reports, datasets, and other assets that contribute to Organizational Memory.

---

# C

## Capability

A repeatable organizational ability.

Capabilities are implemented through Playbooks rather than individual tasks.

Examples include Decision Making, Product Development, Documentation, and Business Development.

---

## Constitution

The enduring principles that guide organizational behavior.

The Constitution provides the ethical and operational foundation of AI Business OS.

It changes rarely.

---

# D

## Decision Gate

A structured evaluation point within a Playbook.

Decision Gates require evidence before execution continues.

Possible outcomes:

- Proceed
- Rework
- Stop
- Escalate

---

# E

## Execution

The coordinated application of organizational capabilities to create value.

Execution transforms knowledge into outcomes.

---

# H

## Human-Led. AI-Amplified.

The foundational philosophy of AI Business OS.

Humans provide purpose, ethics, judgment, and accountability.

AI amplifies consistency, speed, and execution.

---

# K

## Knowledge

Reusable organizational understanding.

Knowledge captures what the organization knows and enables consistent execution.

---

# O

## Organizational Memory

The accumulated experience generated through execution.

Organizational Memory preserves lessons learned and enables continuous improvement.

---

# P

## Playbook

An executable protocol for organizational collaboration.

Playbooks transform Organizational Knowledge into repeatable execution through structured phases, accountable Workers, and evidence-based Decision Gates.

---

## Purpose

The fundamental reason an organization exists.

Purpose guides every strategic and operational decision.

---

# W

## Worker

A Human, AI, or Hybrid role responsible for executing organizational capabilities.

Workers collaborate through Playbooks to achieve organizational outcomes.

---

# Continuous Evolution

The deliberate process of strengthening organizational capability through learning, adaptation, and improvement.

Continuous Evolution closes the Organizational Learning Loop.
