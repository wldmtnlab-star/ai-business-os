# Getting Started

> Build your first AI Business OS in minutes.

This guide walks you through the basic steps for creating your own AI Business OS using the Organization Starter Kit.

You do not need to understand every concept before you begin.

Start simple.

Your organization will evolve over time.

---

# Step 1 — Clone the Repository

Clone or download AI Business OS.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/<your-org>/ai-business-os.git
```

---

# Step 2 — Open the Starter Kit

Navigate to:

```text
examples/
└── _template/
```

This directory contains everything needed to create your own organization.

---

# Step 3 — Create Your Organization

Copy the `_template` directory.

Rename it to your organization's name.

Example:

```text
examples/
├── digital-agency/
├── my-company/
└── _template/
```

---

# Step 4 — Define Your Organization

Complete the documents in this order.

1. Purpose
2. Constitution
3. Operating Model
4. Team
5. Services

These documents establish the foundation of your organization.

---

# Step 5 — Document Your Work

Create Workflows for repeatable processes.

Examples include:

- Customer onboarding
- Product development
- Website redesign
- Incident response
- Hiring

Focus on how work is actually performed.

---

# Step 6 — Capture Learning

After completing real projects, document them as Case Studies.

Each Case Study should improve your:

- Workflows
- Playbooks
- Workers
- Organizational Memory

Execution becomes organizational knowledge.

---

# Step 7 — Continuously Improve

AI Business OS is designed to evolve.

Review and improve your organization regularly.

Update:

- Purpose (rarely)
- Constitution (rarely)
- Operating Model
- Workflows
- Case Studies
- Organizational Memory

Every improvement strengthens future execution.

# Tips

- Start small.
- Document real work.
- Keep knowledge current.
- Improve continuously.
- Let AI amplify—not replace—human judgment.

AI Business OS is not a static framework.

It is an operating system that grows with your organization.
