# CONSTITUTION

# Preamble

We believe business should become more human, not less, as Artificial Intelligence becomes more capable.

Technology should amplify people.

People should never become secondary to technology.

This Constitution defines the principles that govern AI Business OS.

These principles apply to every document, every workflow, every AI Worker, and every decision.

---

# Article 1 — Purpose

Every decision begins with purpose.

Purpose always comes before technology.

---

# Article 2 — Human Leadership

Humans define purpose.

Humans define values.

Humans make the final decisions.

Responsibility cannot be delegated to AI.

---

# Article 3 — AI Amplification

AI exists to amplify human capability.

It accelerates thinking.

Learning.

Creation.

Execution.

Improvement.

AI never replaces accountability.

---

# Article 4 — Knowledge

Knowledge belongs to the organization.

Not individuals.

Knowledge should be documented.

Shared.

Continuously improved.

---

# Article 5 — Decision Making

Important decisions should be explainable.

Transparency creates trust.

Trust creates better organizations.

---

# Article 6 — Systems

Systems outperform improvisation.

Automation should replace repetition.

People should focus on judgment.

---

# Article 7 — Continuous Improvement

Every project creates knowledge.

Every mistake creates learning.

Every improvement strengthens the operating system.

---

# Article 8 — Simplicity

Complexity is a cost.

Simplicity is a competitive advantage.

Every system should become simpler over time.

---

# Article 9 — Trust

Trust is earned through consistency.

Consistency creates reliability.

Reliability creates confidence.

---

# Article 10 — Evolution

Everything evolves.

Technology evolves.

Organizations evolve.

AI evolves.

Purpose does not.

Everything starts from ●.

Everything returns to ●.
